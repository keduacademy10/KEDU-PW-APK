WHAT IS KEDU?

A Free Educational Platform

KEDU is a free educational platform created to help students learn, practice, revise and prepare better.

It brings study materials, lectures, notes, tests, books and other useful educational resources together in one place.

Our goal is to make learning resources simple to discover, easy to access and useful for students throughout their learning journey.

FREE EDUCATION

Learn Without Limits.

KEDU is built with a simple vision — make useful educational resources available to students for free.
